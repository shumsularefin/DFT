## What was built
- `scripts/data/build_elements.py` produces `src/data/elements.json` for Z = 1 to 103 (28.5 KB, 5.3 KB gzipped, byte-identical for the same `--date`, unchanged by Prettier). It uses the method of `gen_element_reference.py`. Fields: symbol, name, Z, mass, period, group, block, category and metal flag, Pauling electronegativity and vdW radius (pymatgen 2026.10.24, S-034), Cordero covalent radius (ASE 3.29.0, S-021 via S-052) and the ICSD 2024 oxidation-state shares (SMACT counts, S-051, state 0 excluded). Gaps stay `null`: the covalent radius of Bk to Lr (found by reading the entries ASE marks `missing`, because Pa's real value is 2.0) and the Pauling value of He, Ne and Ar. `_meta` holds the sources and versions.
- `src/lib/chem/elements.ts` provides the accessors. `geometry.ts` finds every periodic image within a cutoff, with the reach taken from the reciprocal lattice, or works without images in molecule mode. `charge.ts` is the B005 search (D-064). `rules.ts` holds the thresholds and the rule table (sources, decision, learn link). `fixes.ts` holds the pure fixes. `validity.ts` provides `check(structure, mode, { symmetry? })`, `exportGate` and `withOverrideComment`.
- Each `Issue` has: `code`, `severity`, `title`, `why` (plain language), `atoms`, `fix`, `alternatives`, `learn` (module plus a concept id in `concepts.json`), `heuristic`, `sources` and `data` (the numbers behind the message).
- Rules:
  - **B001 / B002:** below 0.5 / 0.75 of the covalent-radius sum, closest image of each pair only. Same element: merge to the midpoint, with delete one and move apart as alternatives. Different elements: move apart to the radius sum, or delete one. An atom overlapping its own image gets no fix. Elements without a radius are skipped with an info.
  - **B003:** half-radius spheres. The fix scales the cell until the closest pair reaches 0.75 of its sum; it is not offered while two atoms coincide.
  - **B004:** angle outside 20 to 160 degrees, or below 1 Angstrom^3 per atom. A bad angle or a zero volume skips the distance rules.
  - **B005:** common states first (info), then rare states (warning naming them and their shares), else an error. Single elements and all-metal systems get an info.
  - **B006 / B007:** Pauling's first and second rules (D-065).
  - **B008:** reuses `evaluatePerovskite` with the S-003 coordination policy (D-067).
  - **B009:** Hume-Rothery checks, using covalent radii as a stated stand-in. The crystal-structure rule is reported as not checked.
  - **B010:** wrap info; same-species duplicates within 0.1 Angstrom are an error with "merge duplicates".
  - **B011:** space group and Wyckoff letters from the M5.1 result, or "not analysed".
  - **B012:** molecule mode note (valence comes from RDKit, M10.3).
- Export: any error blocks export until the user gives a non-empty reason. That reason becomes `OVERRIDE <codes>: <reason>` on the structure's comment line, which the POSCAR writer emits as line 1.

- **Rock-salt NaCl** (`kpaths.json`): no error or warning; B005 gives `Na+ Cl-`.
- **"Na2Cl":** B005 error.
- **Two Na atoms 0.4 Angstrom apart:** one B001 error. The merge fix leaves one atom at the midpoint and clears B001; delete and move apart also work. The input is unchanged.
- **30 Na atoms in 10 Angstrom^3:** B003 error. After the scale fix, B001 to B003 are clear. With coincident atoms, no scale fix is offered.

## Decisions made (D-nnn) and open questions raised (Q-nnn)
- **D-063 (geometry rules):**
  - The work order says "merge only within 0.1 Angstrom", but its own test merges at 0.4 Angstrom; D-063 records how this is resolved.
  - B003 uses half-radius spheres, because full spheres flag real crystals: SrTiO3 fills 0.87 of its cell and `bct_I41amd_test` 1.15.
  - D-063 also records the export override.
- **D-064:** charge search and ranking.
- **D-065:** Pauling-rule conventions (shell factor 1.2, ionic Delta chi of at least 1.0, CN VI radii, warn below the limit only).
- **D-066:** Hume-Rothery conventions.
- **D-067:** B008 severity. M8.2 warns for any t above 1.0, which would flag cubic SrTiO3. B008 therefore warns on t only outside S-003's measured window, and still quotes the M8 message. A note about this was added to Q-005.
