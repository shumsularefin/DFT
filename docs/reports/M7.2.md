## What was built
- Eight topics in `src/content/handbook/05-practical-choices/`: `plane-waves-and-encut` (ENCUT lab), `kpoints-and-the-brillouin-zone` (k-mesh lab plus the BZ lab via a new `also` prop on `<Lab>`), `pseudopotentials-and-paw`, `functionals`, `smearing`, `spin-and-magnetism`, `dft-plus-u`, `vdw-corrections` (no lab).

- Labs: `smearing` (computed), `dft-plus-u` (computed, toy), `paw-picture`, `functional-ladder`, `spin-cartoon` (all illustrative, with visible caveats).
- Tag cards: a static `/tags/` page built from `src/data/vasp-tags.json` (summary, why this value, default, typical, what breaks, corrections, related, sources, VASP version) and an inline `<Tag name="ENCUT" />` component for topics that fails the build for an unknown tag. Linked from the footer. Every "why this value" mention of ENCUT, KPOINTS (KSPACING), ISMEAR, SIGMA, ISPIN, MAGMOM and the LDAU tags in module 5 links to its card.
- 65 questions in `module-05.json` (16 concepts, at least 4 each, all published); glossary: brillouin-zone, pulay-stress, smearing, kpoint.
- `LabShell`: the "What to notice" aside now carries the lab title in its label, so two labs on one page stay axe clean (landmark-unique).
