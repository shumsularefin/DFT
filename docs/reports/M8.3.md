## Science traceability
No new science. Every number shown is a Shannon entry (S-001) or computed by the M8.2 library (S-002, S-003, S-022). Text ranges are labelled rules of thumb (Q-005).

## Notes
- The periodic-table component's keyboard behaviour is tested through the tool's e2e and its layout logic by unit tests; there is no separate component test harness in the repo.
- Elements like O have only anion entries in the Shannon table, so they are struck through in the A and B pickers.
