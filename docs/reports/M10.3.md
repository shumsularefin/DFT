## What was built
- **`@rdkit/rdkit` 2026.9.1** (exact pin, as listed in D-002 and SPEC).
  - It loads only in `src/workers/rdkit.worker.ts`, through `locateFile` and the wasm `?url`.
  - `src/lib/chem/rdkit-client.ts` creates the worker on the first request and reports a plain message if Web Workers or WebAssembly are blocked.
  - The request logic is in `src/lib/chem/molecule-service.ts` and is tested in Node against the same RDKit build (`rdkit-node.ts`, tests only).
- **`src/lib/chem/molgraph.ts`:**
  - Types: atoms (element, formal charge, H count) and bonds (1, 2, 3, aromatic).
  - Conversion: graph to SMILES with the atom order kept, `graphFromRdkitJson`, `parseMolecule` (SMILES or MOL block), `checkGraph`, `toSmiles`, `toMolblock`.
  - Sanitization errors become B012 issues on the offending atom: too many bonds, cannot kekulize, syntax errors, plus a generic fallback.
  - Drawing and layout: `layout2d` (CoordGen through `set_new_coords(true)`, falling back to `set_new_coords()`) and `depictSvg`.
  - `expandHydrogens` turns H counts into atoms.
- **`src/lib/chem/geom3d.ts`** (no RDKit) has these terms (conventions in D-070):
  - harmonic bonds to Cordero single-bond sums;
  - VSEPR angle terms from the steric number, with lone pairs from the valence electrons;
  - a soft repulsion;
  - planarity terms at trigonal centres and across double and aromatic bonds.

  The start is the 2D layout plus seeded out-of-plane offsets. The minimizer is L-BFGS with an analytic gradient and an Armijo line search. Results are deterministic for a seed and labelled "Rough starting geometry for DFT, not a quantum-chemical optimization."
- **`src/lib/chem/molexport.ts`:**
  - `writeXyz`.
  - `writeMolBlock`: V2000, 3D, aromatic bonds as type 4, `M  CHG` lines.
  - `moleculeInBox`: a cubic cell with at least 10 Angstrom of vacuum by default, configurable. Notes say Gamma-only k-points and ISIF = 2 (V023).
- **`writePoscar`** gained `coordinates: 'cartesian'`.
- **Check page `/design/molecule-check/`** (`MoleculeCheck.tsx`), following the M5.1 symmetry-check precedent:
  - It reads SMILES or a MOL block and shows the canonical SMILES, the layout method and the drawing.
  - It explains B012 errors.
  - It builds the rough 3D geometry (marked Heuristic) and offers it as XYZ and as a POSCAR in a box.
  - RDKit is downloaded only when a button is pressed. The molecule editor itself is M10.5.
- **Lazy budget `rdkit`** in `budgets.json`: files containing `set_new_coords` (the wasm and the worker), capped at 2500 KB gzipped.

Test values (the targets are the model's own definitions, as the work order says):
- **Methane:** four C-H bonds at 1.07 +- 0.01 Angstrom (0.31 + 0.76), and all six H-C-H angles at 109.47 +- 1 degree.
- **CO2:** linear within 1 degree.
- **Ethene:** all six atoms within 0.05 Angstrom of a plane.
- **Benzene:** the ring within 0.05 Angstrom of a plane.
- **Water:** H-O-H between 100 and 110 degrees (109.47 in this model).
- **Valence error:** `C(C)(C)(C)(C)C` and the equivalent graph give one B012 error on atom 0, with "a neutral carbon has four" and RDKit's text. The unsanitized graph is kept.
- **Determinism:** the same seed gives identical coordinates.
- **Also tested:**
  - aspirin, caffeine, ibuprofen and carbazole converge, with planar aromatic rings;
  - round trips SMILES to graph to SMILES and MOL block to graph;
  - the 3D MOL block read back by RDKit;
  - `M  CHG`;
  - the box spacing in Direct and Cartesian (POSCAR parsed back);
  - the client's failure paths.

## Not done / deferred (be exact)
- **No molecule editor** (M10.5). The check page only proves the engine.
- **No bond-order correction:** double and triple bonds come out too long, and the result says so (Q-045).
- **Rotation about single bonds is not optimized** (no torsion term). Conformations come from the 2D start.
- **Atoms with more than four electron domains, and transition metals, get no angle term.** A note says so.
- **The service-worker caching of the wasm is covered by the existing `/_astro/` CacheFirst rule** (`scripts/build/sw.mjs`). No test exercises it offline; that is M11.1.
