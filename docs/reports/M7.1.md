## Mixing behaviour observed (recorded, not guessed)
Linear mixing, start from the bare-well orbital, stop at residual 1e-12:

| alpha | 0.05 | 0.1 | 0.3 | 0.5 | 0.7 | 0.8 | 0.9 | 1.0 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Z = 2, iterations | > 400 (still falling) | 217 | 64 | 33 | 19 | 15 | 11 | 14 |
| Z = 1, iterations | > 400 (still falling) | 215 | 64 | 34 | 21 | 30 | 47 | does not converge |

Z = 1 at alpha = 1.0: every update reverses direction; the residual falls to about 1e-10 near iteration 50, then grows again and settles into a two-state swing with residual about 0.445 (checked to iteration 400). The lab calls this "oscillating" (heuristic: at least half of the last 20 updates reversed and the residual did not halve). The tests assert alpha = 0.3 converges, alpha = 1.0 oscillates for Z = 1 and converges in 14 steps for Z = 2, alpha = 0.05 is slow, and that the fastest alpha is 0.9 (Z = 2) and 0.7 (Z = 1), because the topic quotes those.
