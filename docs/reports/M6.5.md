## Quota arithmetic (Spark; figures from Q-006, still unconfirmed: 50 000 reads, 20 000 writes, 20 000 deletes per day, 1 GiB stored)
Per signed-in person per day, worst realistic case:
- Reads: one `getDoc` per visit to `/progress/` (sign-in or resume). 20 visits: 20 reads.
- Writes: at most one on opening `/progress/` when something changed since the last sync, at most one per 60 s while the page stays open and the state keeps changing (changes on that page come only from import, reset or settings), plus one when the tab is hidden. 20 visits with changes: about 40 writes.
- Deletes: at most 1.
- Storage: one document, typically 10 to 60 KB (16 concepts, 76 questions, history capped at 200 events per concept); the cap keeps it under 900 000 characters by construction for the planned 71 concepts only if histories stay short, so the engine refuses to write above the limit and says so.
- So about 1 000 daily active syncing people before writes hit 20 000 per day, 2 500 before reads hit 50 000. The absolute worst case (the page left open for 8 hours with a change every minute, which the UI does not produce on its own) is 480 writes, or about 40 such people.
