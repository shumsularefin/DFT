## What was built
- **`scripts/reference/gen_structure_tools_reference.py`** writes `tests/reference/structure_tools_reference.json`, with pymatgen 2026.10.24, spglib 2.8.0, numpy 2.5.4 and scipy 1.18.1 recorded in `_meta`. It contains:
  - **Slabs:** pymatgen's own `SlabGenerator` with its defaults (`primitive=True`, `ftol=0.1`, symmetric duplicates filtered) for the six slabs below, at 5, 10 and 15 Angstrom. It records the termination count and, for each termination, the atom count, surface area, `is_polar()` and `is_symmetric()`. Nominal oxidation states are typed into the script.
  - **Orbits:** spglib `equivalent_atoms` for all 39 golden structures.
  - **Defect pairs:** brute force over every pair of distinct sites, grouped by spglib's operations acting as site permutations, for three cells: NaCl conventional, fcc Cu 2x2x2 and rutile.
- **`src/lib/crystal/slab.ts`:**
  - `slabPlan(api, s, hkl)` returns:
    - the in-plane pair (shortest, reduced, right-handed);
    - the stacking vector, normal, bulk period, input (hkl) spacing and area;
    - the layers, the possible cuts and the distinct terminations (with the bottom and top layer species).
  - `buildSlab(api, s, hkl, options)` returns the slab in a standard frame (a1 along x, c along the normal), with `symmetric`, a `polarity` object and notes. The options are `layers` or `minThicknessA`, `vacuumA`, `center`, `termination` and `fixBottomLayers`.
  - The `polarity` object holds `polar`, the Tasker type, the dipole per area, the charges, `heuristic: true`, a message and the sources. A polar slab's message advises a symmetric slab or a dipole correction (LDIPOL, IDIPOL; S-066, S-065).
- **`src/lib/crystal/defects.ts`:**
  - `distinctSites` (moyo orbits, Wyckoff letters, site symmetries)
  - `vacancy` and `substitute`
  - `defectPairs` (classes by symmetry, with multiplicity and member pairs)
  - `shortestLatticeVector`, `suggestSupercell` (default 10 Angstrom, labelled a convention) and `defectSupercell`
  - `exchangedAtoms` and `neutralFormationEnergy` (S-067; every result says charged defects and corrections are out of scope)

What the tests check:
- **Slabs (work order):** Cu (100), (110), (111), NaCl (100), (111) and rutile TiO2 (110).
  - Termination counts equal pymatgen's (1, 1, 1, 1, 1, 2).
  - Surface areas are within 1e-6 of pymatgen's, both in the plan and in the built cell.
  - The input (hkl) spacing matches pymatgen's `d_hkl`.
  - At 5, 10 and 15 Angstrom, the multiset of (atoms, polar, symmetric) over all terminations equals pymatgen's. For rutile (110) that is one symmetric non-polar slab and one polar asymmetric slab at each thickness.
- **Polarity (work order):** NaCl (111) is flagged polar, Tasker III, with the LDIPOL and IDIPOL advice and S-063. NaCl (100) is not polar (Tasker I).
- **Geometry:**
  - The vacuum is exactly the gap to the next image.
  - The slab is centred and c is along z.
  - Four Cu (111) layers span 3 a / sqrt(3).
  - The fcc (111) in-plane pair is two nearest-neighbour vectors at 60 or 120 degrees.
  - `fixBottomLayers: 2` freezes exactly the bottom two layers.
  - (2 2 2) reduces to (1 1 1), and (0 0 0) is refused.
- **Orbits (work order):** for all 39 golden structures, the orbit partition equals spglib's `equivalent_atoms`, not only the count.
- **Defect pairs:** class count, total pair count and every class's distance, species and multiplicity equal the brute-force result for the three cells. For example, Cu 2x2x2 up to 3.7 Angstrom gives 192 + 48 pairs in 2 classes.
- **Supercell:**
  - fcc Cu (conventional input, 10 Angstrom) gives 16 cells (64 atoms) at 2 sqrt(2) a. The test comment derives this by hand from Hermite's bound in three dimensions.
  - For three lower-symmetry cells, the reported image distance equals a brute-force shortest vector.
  - The diagonal fallback is tested.
- **Formation energy (work order):** two hand-checked integer examples (vacancy: 3 eV; substitution with two reservoirs: 3 eV), and non-finite input is refused.

## Not done / deferred (be exact)
- **No UI** (M10.4).
- **Not in this stage:** interfaces, heterostructures and SQS.
- **Charged defects** are out of scope: no Fermi-level term and no finite-size corrections.
- **No non-stoichiometric slabs:** there are no symmetrized slabs that remove atoms from one surface (pymatgen's `symmetrize=True`), so a polar surface can only be flagged, not repaired.
- **No bond-aware cuts:** pymatgen's `bonds` and `max_broken_bonds` options are not offered, so cuts that break a bond are not filtered out.
- **The supercell search stops at 64 cells,** then falls back to a diagonal supercell.
