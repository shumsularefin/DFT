## What was built
- `units.ts`: kBar to GPa, eV/A^3 to GPa, THz to cm^-1 and meV, cm^-1 to meV, Ry and Hartree to eV, eV/atom to kJ/mol, eV/A to nN, each computed from `constants.ts` (CODATA 2018, exact SI constants; `AVOGADRO_PER_MOL` added) and SI definitions. `frequencyFromThz` gives all three units.
- `parseOutcar` reads the `f` and `f/i` lines after `Eigenvectors and eigenvalues of the dynamical matrix` (layout from the VASP wiki, S-019, oldid 36045), at most 3 NIONS of them, and warns when the heading has no mode lines.
- `src/lib/vasp/phonons.ts`: `analyseGammaModes` (signed frequencies, the three lowest |nu| as acoustic, imaginary optical and imaginary acoustic lists, near-zero acoustic count against `ACOUSTIC_NEAR_ZERO_CM1 = 5`, a check that the printed cm^-1 column matches THz times the project factor).
- Review tool: `POST-PHONON-IMAG` (computed) warns on any imaginary mode outside the acoustic three and names it in THz, cm^-1 and meV; `POST-PHONON-ACOUSTIC` (Heuristic, Q-058) warns unless all three acoustic modes are near zero. Fixes quote S-042 (EDIFF 1E-7 to 1E-8, ADDGRID, ENCUT and k-mesh; displace along the soft mode).
- Golden data: `scripts/reference/gen_phonon_reference.py` writes `tests/reference/phonon_reference.json` (unit factors from scipy.constants) and `tests/reference/phonon/OUTCAR_phonon_{stable,unstable}`, read back by ASE `Vasp.read_vib_freq` (pymatgen has no reader for these lines).
