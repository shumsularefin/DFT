## What was built
- `src/lib/optics/derived.ts`: `opticalPoint(E, eps1, eps2)` gives |eps|, n, k, alpha (1/cm), R (normal incidence), L = eps2/|eps|^2 and sigma (S/m); `deriveOptics` does this for xx, yy, zz and for the average of eps (eps averaged first, `AVERAGE_RULE`), and adds `DRUDE_NOTE` when the caller says the system is a metal (no intraband term, S-042).
- `src/lib/physics/constants.ts`: `SPEED_OF_LIGHT_M_S` (exact) and `VACUUM_PERMITTIVITY_F_M` (CODATA 2018, the generator asserts it equals scipy's value within 1e-9 relative).
- `src/lib/vasp/vasprunDielectric.ts`: an indexOf-based reader (no DOM, linear in the file) for every `<dielectricfunction>` block, real and imaginary parts for xx yy zz xy yz zx, with the density-density block selected when VASP 6 prints both.
- `src/lib/vasp/outcar.ts`: `epsilonInf` (block "including local field effects in DFT"), `epsilonIon` ("IONIC CONTRIBUTION") and `born` (BORN EFFECTIVE CHARGES), each with a warning naming the line when a block cannot be read.
- `src/lib/optics/dielectric.ts`: `staticDielectric` (eps_inf + eps_ion) and `bornSumRule` (largest |sum of Z*| component against 0.1, S-042, labelled Heuristic).
- Golden data: `tests/reference/optics_reference.json`, `tests/reference/optics/vasprun_dielectric.xml` (two blocks, read back by pymatgen `Vasprun._parse_diel`), `OUTCAR_lepsilon_sum_ok` and `OUTCAR_lepsilon_sum_broken` (read back by pymatgen `read_lepsilon` and `read_lepsilon_ionic`). The eps grid is a synthetic Lorentz oscillator, not a calculation.
