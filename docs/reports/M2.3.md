# M2.3 Module 2: Quantum basics without math (with labs)

Status: done        Branch: claude/vigilant-newton-ws593b        PR: see docs/PROGRESS.md

## What was built
- Six topics in `src/content/handbook/02-quantum-basics/` with the nine-part anatomy and three levels: `particles-and-waves`, `the-wavefunction`, `particle-in-a-box` (lab 1), `orbitals` (lab 2), `spin-and-the-pauli-principle`, `why-energy-levels-matter-for-materials` (bands, gap and DOS in one paragraph, no forward links). Only the two lab topics have a lab; the others set `noLab: true`.
- `src/lib/physics/constants.ts` (hbar^2/2m, Rydberg energy, Bohr radius, h, from CODATA 2018, S-031), `quadrature.ts` (Simpson, radial substitution), `box.ts` (energies, wavefunctions, beat period), `hydrogen.ts` (Laguerre radial functions, real harmonics l <= 3, seeded sampler), each with tests.
- Golden file `tests/reference/quantum_basics_reference.json`, generated by `scripts/reference/gen_quantum_basics_reference.py` with scipy (never hand-edited); listed in `tests/reference/README.md`.
- Sampling worker (`orbital.worker.ts`, `orbital-sampler.ts`): superseded requests are dropped, buffers are transferred, main-thread fallback if Worker is unavailable.
- Lab `particle-in-a-box` (`BoxLab.tsx`, SVG, badge Computed): sliders for L (0.5 to 10 A), n (1 to 6), mix and time within the beat, readouts for E_n, interior nodes, gap and beat period, a Play toggle with a printed slowdown factor (hidden under reduced motion), one-electron caveat, text description.
- Lab `orbitals` (`OrbitalsLab.tsx`, `OrbitalScene.ts`, badge Computed): 30 hydrogenic orbitals (n <= 4, labels like 2p_z, 3d_xy), 3D point cloud, optional radial-node shells, 50 000 point mode, readouts for energy, most probable distance, radial nodes. Without WebGL it shows the radial probability curve. "One electron only" caveat is in the lab notice.
- 31 questions (`module-02.json`, 4 to 6 per topic, 5 numeric), 9 glossary terms (total 64), D-032, Q-024, Q-025.

## Worker responsiveness (documented measurement)
In `module-02.spec.ts` a requestAnimationFrame counter runs while the "Denser cloud" switch makes the worker sample 50 000 points and the scene rebuild. Headless Chromium here has software WebGL, so frame rate is low in any case. Results: 8 to 13 frames over the window with a longest gap of 133 to 183 ms; the test asserts more than 3 frames and a longest gap below 500 ms. Sampling does not block the main thread (it runs in the worker); the gaps come from software rendering of 50 000 points. Not measured on real touch devices.
