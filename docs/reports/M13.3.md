## What was built
- **`scripts/reference/gen_vasp_output_fixtures.py`:** a new `outcar_magnetization_fixture`.
  - It writes two synthetic OUTCAR fragments: `OUTCAR_mag_spd` has two `magnetization (x)` tables (the reader must keep the last) and a `number of electron ... magnetization` line; `OUTCAR_mag_spdf` has an f column.
  - The expected values come from pymatgen `Outcar(...).magnetization` and `total_mag` (pymatgen 2026.10.31 in the reference venv).
  - Rerunning the generator left every older fixture byte-identical: only `expected.json` gained lines.
- **`src/lib/vasp/outcar.ts`:** `siteMagnetization` (`MagnetizationTable`) with the column names from the header, per-site values, `tot`, and the footer row.
  - It keeps the last table. A missing tot column gives the orbital sum; a short row keeps the columns it has.
- **`src/lib/magnetism/analysis.ts`:**
  - `rankEnergies`: eV per magnetic ion relative to FM (or to the lowest, with a warning), sorted, with a near-tie note under 1 meV.
  - `patternKept`: per-site verdicts (kept, flipped, collapsed, shrunk, appeared, not checked). It always carries `heuristic: true` and the rule text.
  - `totalMomentCheck`: compares the site sum with the cell moment. Heuristic, with a tolerance of 0.5 μB per cell.
- **`src/lib/magnetism/heisenberg.ts`:**
  - `HeisenbergConvention`: the sign, pairs counted once or twice, and unit spins or length S.
  - `describeConvention`, the text printed with every J.
  - `shellCoefficients`, built on `bondStatistics`.
  - `fitExchange`: one-sided Jacobi SVD, residuals, RMS, condition number, rank, and the under-, exactly- and rank-deficient warnings.
- **UI:** an Analyse tab on `/tools/magnetic/` (`AnalysePanel.tsx`, lazy loaded, and `analyseModel.ts`, which is pure).
  - It works on the set from the last build tab, so the intended MAGMOM is the one the user downloaded.
  - Per configuration: drop OSZICAR and/or OUTCAR (read locally), or type the energy, and choose F or E0.
  - Output:
    - a ranking table and an SVG bar chart (with a text alternative);
    - per-site tables (intended MAGMOM, final moment, verdict, Heuristic badge, file and line);
    - the total-moment check;
    - the convention box (always visible) and the J table, whose caption carries the convention;
    - a statement that no Néel or Curie temperature is estimated.
