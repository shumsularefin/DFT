Status: done        Branch: claude/vigilant-newton-ws593b        PR: see docs/PROGRESS.md

## What was built
- Page `/tools/kpath/` (`KPathTool.tsx`): (1) cell input: 14-Bravais chooser with plain-language validation, or POSCAR/CIF text and file drop (parsed locally, 25 MB cap), plus hand-off from the Input Builder and the structure Builder; (2) "What happened to your cell" panel with the space group and the standardization; (3) convention switch (HPKOT default, Setyawan-Curtarolo) with a paragraph on why they differ and the Q-013 caveat; (4) path table with segment length in 1/Angstrom (2 pi included), points per segment or density, per-segment override, total; (5) lazy 3D Brillouin zone with a marker slider, Play button (hidden under reduced motion), axes and mesh toggles, labelled "Illustrative"; (6) downloads: KPOINTS, KPATH.in, KPOINTS_OPT (route A/B note), CSV, JSON, POSCAR_primitive.
- `src/lib/vasp/kpathExport.ts` (pure): `buildPathTable`, `pathPoints`, writers. `src/lib/crystal/bravais.ts`: the 14 lattices. `BzScene.setMarker`. Input Builder k-points step links to the tool. Decision D-050.
