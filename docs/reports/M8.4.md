## Science and honesty

- Map boundaries: mu = sqrt(2) - 1 and sqrt(3) - 1 (S-022) and t from 0.825 to 1.059 (S-003, decision tree, 74 % on 576 compounds). Labelled Heuristic, with the paper's own caveat about edge- and face-sharing octahedra. Q-005 updated.
- Tau threshold 4.18 with the confirmation note (Q-003, arXiv text only).
- Gaps are drawn as crosses with the reason, empty in the CSV, never interpolated.
