## What was built
- `src/lib/perovskite/factors.ts`: `goldschmidt`, `octahedral`, `bartelTau` (threshold 4.18). When rA = rB, tau returns `ok: false`, reason `rA equals rB`, and no number. When rA < rB it evaluates and sets `sitesLookSwapped`; nothing is swapped. `TAU_STATUS` records the version of the paper checked.
- `src/lib/perovskite/sites.ts`: `siteAverage(occupants)` for mixed sites. Each occupant has its own element, oxidation state, CN, spin and geometry. Occupancies must be in (0, 1] and sum to 1 (tolerance 1e-6). Radius and oxidation state are averaged with occupancy weights. Any missing radius fails the whole site, with the lookup's reason (for example `spin required`). `MIXING_CONVENTION` is the label for the UI.
- `src/lib/chem/oxidation.ts` and `src/data/oxidation-states.json`:
  - The data file holds SMACT 4.0.2 ICSD 2024 counts for 96 elements (7 KB), built by `scripts/data/build_oxidation.py` with the same method as `gen_element_reference.py`. The output is byte-identical for the same `--date`.
  - `classifyOxidation` gives `common` / `known-rare` / `not-observed` (D-061).
  - Helpers: `oxidationShares`, `commonOxidationStates`.
- `src/lib/perovskite/checks.ts`: `evaluatePerovskite({ A, B, X })` returns the site results, the charge sum, the factors (only when every radius exists) and a list of checks. Each check has an id, level, message, `heuristic`, sources and an optional `swap-a-b` action:
  - missing radius and invalid site input: error;
  - charge balance `nA + nB + 3 nX = 0` with mixed-site averages: error;
  - uncommon or unobserved oxidation state: heuristic warning (S-051);
  - A not at CN 12 or B not at CN 6: info (S-003 Methods);
  - mixed site: info with the convention label;
  - sites look swapped: warning with the action;
  - tau undefined: warning;
  - octahedral factor against sqrt(2) - 1 and sqrt(3) - 1: heuristic (S-022, S-003);
  - Goldschmidt t against "about 0.8 to 1.0, near 1 cubic, below tilted, above about 1 hexagonal": heuristic rule of thumb (Q-005), quoting S-003's measured window 0.825 < t < 1.059 and its 74 % accuracy for comparison.
- `scripts/reference/gen_perovskite_reference.py`: status text updated and the generator re-run; only `_meta.tau_status` changed in the golden file (diff checked: 1 line).

## Q-003: the tau formula against the paper
arxiv.org was reachable. I read the paper as arXiv:1801.07700v2; PMC returned a captcha page and science.org returned 403, so the published text was not compared. What the paper says:
- **Equation 2:** tau = rX/rB - nA (nA - (rA/rB) / ln(rA/rB)), "where nA is the oxidation state of A, ri is the ionic radius of ion i, rA > rB by definition and tau < 4.18 indicates perovskite". This matches the planned formula and the golden generator exactly.
- **Materials and Methods, "Radii assignment":** Shannon radii for A and B are taken at the coordination closest to 12 and 6, and the larger cation is assigned to A.
- **Double perovskites:** rB is approximated as the arithmetic mean of the two B radii; the geometric mean changes 14 of 918 labels.
- **Ranges quoted:** 0.414 < mu < 0.732 (geometric); decision-tree window 0.825 < t < 1.059, where t is right for 74 % of 576 compounds.
- **Not stated:** the anion coordination number, and how to treat a mixed A site or a non-integer nA.

Result: `TAU_STATUS.confirmed = true`, so the "formula not yet confirmed" note is not needed. The status names the version checked so that M8.3 can show it.
