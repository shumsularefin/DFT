## What was built
- `dft-plus-u`:
  - Four ways to choose U: database values, linear response with `LDAUTYPE = 3`, an empirical fit, a U scan. Each one says why you would pick it.
  - The Materials Project Ueff table, with its four conditions: GGA (PBE)+U only, Dudarev on d orbitals with `LDAUJ = 0`, only when O or F is present, and fitted to formation enthalpies. Sources are S-034, S-080 and S-081, per Q-051 and D-087.
  - The S-042 pitfalls: mixing +U and GGA energies, metastable states and U ramping, per-species order, `LDAUPRINT`.
  - The old sentence "this site does not list U values" is removed.
- `functionals`:
  - SCAN versus r2SCAN stability, and the rVV10 `BPARAM` values (15.7 and 11.95).
  - mBJ gives no total energy and no forces.
  - The zero-weight hybrid band route (VASPKIT 303, 251, 252, 911), with a reason on each step. The same route is used for meta-GGA bands.
  - Optics with HSE, and the scissor-shift compromise.
- New topic `12-properties/other-calculations`:
  - All 19 rows of the S-042 table, each with its run and its key settings.
  - A formula table with eight formulas. The defect finite-size term cites S-067.
  - Links to the existing tools: the planner (formation enthalpy, surface energy), the work-function tool (work function, Δρ, band offsets), and Studio (hull).
  - New concept `property-catalogue` and 4 questions (`q-m12-oc-1` to `-4`).
- `scf-trouble`: how to tell which loop is failing, the 9-rung electronic ladder, and "Signs of a good calculation".
- `relaxation-trouble`: the 7-rung ionic ladder, which links to the signs.
- The home page count is now 72 topics, and the module-12 e2e spec includes the new topic.
