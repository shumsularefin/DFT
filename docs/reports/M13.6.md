## What was built
- **`scripts/reference/gen_eos_reference.py`** writes `tests/reference/eos_reference.json`. Rerunning it gives a byte-identical file.
  - Three illustrative parameter sets × two forms, with three samplings each: clean with 7 points (0.94 to 1.06), clean with 11 points (0.90 to 1.10), and noisy with 11 points (2 meV seeded noise).
  - Energies come from pymatgen's own `_func`.
  - Every set is fitted twice: by pymatgen `EOS` and by scipy `curve_fit` (LM, tightened). The scipy fit also gives standard errors and RMS.
  - Four bad-sampling fixtures: minimum outside the range, 4 points, asymmetric, and 30 meV noise.
  - The unit constant comes from `scipy.constants`.
- **`src/lib/physics/constants.ts`:** `EV_PER_A3_IN_GPA = e × 1e30 / 1e9` = 160.21766 GPa.
- **`src/lib/physics/eos.ts`:**
  - Third-order Birch-Murnaghan (S-061) and Murnaghan (S-062), with analytic parameter derivatives.
  - Parabola start; Levenberg-Marquardt fit (S-068).
  - Standard errors as in `curve_fit`; residuals and RMS (per cell and per atom).
  - Warnings, each with a severity, a `heuristic` flag and its sources. The full list is in D-071.
  - `cubicLatticeConstant` (with error propagation) and `eosCurve`.
- **`src/lib/vasp/outcar.ts`:** reads the last `volume of cell` value.
- **`src/lib/vasp/eosInput.ts`:**
  - **OUTCAR:** reads volume, energy(sigma->0) and NIONS, and warns when a relaxation did not converge.
  - **OSZICAR:** gives the energy only, with a "type the volume" note. When a folder has both files, the OUTCAR wins.
  - **CSV:** with or without a header; the `collect.sh` header works.
  - Rows are turned into points, with the mixed-atom-count check.
  - Result CSV with units.
- **`src/lib/vasp/series.ts`:**
  - **Volume series:** `volumeSeries(structure, { fractions })` scales the cell isotropically. The default is 7 points from 0.94 to 1.06. `cellVolume` gives the volume.
  - **Cubic check and INCAR:** `looksCubic` is a heuristic that presets the cubic choice. `eosEntries` sets ISIF = 4, or 2 for cubic cells, with IBRION = 2, NSW = 100, EDIFF = 1E-6, EDIFFG = -0.005 and a fixed ENCUT, following S-042.
  - **Project:** `buildEosProject` writes `01_eos/vol_<f>/` folders and a `collect.sh` that prints label, volume, energy and nions. The README explains the ISIF choice, the Pulay stress and ISTART = 2.
  - `run_all.sh` and `make_potcar.sh` are reused, with a root argument. No POTCAR content is written.
- **Input Builder, series step:** a "Series type" choice: convergence tests, or equation of state.
  - The EOS mode has the volume fractions, a "cell is cubic" box preset by the heuristic, ENCUT and mesh fields shared across every volume, and validation.
  - It downloads `eos-series.zip`.
- **`/tools/eos/`** (`EosTool.tsx`, `EosPlot.tsx`, `eos.css`):
  - **Input:** file drop (OUTCAR or OSZICAR, by folder), CSV paste, an editable table, and sample data with a "synthetic" banner.
  - **Settings:** form (BM3 or Murnaghan), atom count, and the cubic cell type for the lattice constant.
  - **Results:** every number carries its unit and standard error. The warnings show a "Heuristic:" prefix where it applies.
  - **Plot:** E(V) with the fitted curve, the V0 marker and a residual panel in meV, keyboard navigable.
  - **Export and links:** SVG, PNG and CSV export, and a text table. The page is linked from `/tools/` and from the command palette.

## Decisions made (D-nnn) and open questions raised (Q-nnn)
- D-071 covers the equation-of-state conventions:
  - the LM details and stopping rules;
  - the test tolerances, with the reason a 1e-6 match to scipy was dropped;
  - the warning thresholds;
  - the default fractions and folder names;
  - the INCAR choice and the cubic heuristic;
  - cells per conventional cell;
  - the volume source.
- No new open question. The heuristic thresholds (asymmetry ratio 2, 2 meV per atom) are labelled "Heuristic" in the UI and recorded in D-071.

Note on the test tolerance: the extra check "within 1e-6 of scipy" failed on B0' by 1.3e-6.
- B0' has a standard error of 1 to 2 on these sets, so its valley is flat. Fits whose χ² agree to 1e-12 can differ there by about 1e-6.
- Our χ² is at or below both scipy's and pymatgen's in every case.
- The test now requires χ² no larger than scipy's plus agreement within 1e-5. The work order's 1e-4 against pymatgen is unchanged.

## Not done / deferred (be exact)
- **Elastic constants, phonons and thermal expansion:** not in this stage, by the work order.
- **No E-V comparison of several phases or magnetic states on one plot.** The handbook mentions this use; the tool fits one curve at a time.
- **No automatic cell detection for the lattice constant.** The user picks simple/conventional, bcc primitive or fcc primitive. The tool does not read a POSCAR on this page.
- **The Input Builder's "cubic" preset is a geometric heuristic,** not a symmetry analysis. The user can change it.
- **The Murnaghan form is not compared with pymatgen in the browser e2e test** (Birch-Murnaghan only). It is covered by the unit tests.
