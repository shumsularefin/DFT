## What was built

- **One engine (M16.1, D-108).** `src/lib/viewer3d/`: a pure quaternion arcball (`arcball.ts`, no pole lock, tested through the pole), `ArcballControls` used by every view, VESTA-style views along a, b, c, a\*, b\*, c\*, any [uvw] and the normal of any (hkl) (`views.ts`), and `Viewer3D` drawing the plain-data layers of `types.ts` (atoms, lines with tubes, curves, arrows with solid or open heads, meshes with colour maps, points, labels) in perspective or orthographic, on demand, with picking, HTML labels, theme colours, reduced motion, pause when hidden, context-loss recovery and a full dispose.
- **The stage beside the text (M16.2, D-109, D-111).** `TopicStage.astro` and `stageApp.ts`: a live stage column beside every topic, with Split, Focus 3D and Text only, a splitter, Pin, step buttons and the reading position choosing the state. It is server-rendered with an SVG poster drawn at build time from the same scene, boots after load when idle (or at once on a touch), and keeps three.js out of the initial JavaScript.
- **Stages in front matter (M16.3, D-110).** Each topic has a `stage:` block (scene, 3 to 5 states with section, params, layers, camera, caption), checked by `stageSchema`, `stages.test.ts` and `scripts/guard/check-3d-stages.mjs`, which is strict since M16.15.
- **Scenes for every topic (M16.5 to M16.14).** 85 scenes in 19 scene files (`scenes/m00.ts` to `m12b.ts`), each a pure generator with parameters, an honest badge, sources and a generated description, unit-tested in Node. All 83 topics have a stage.
- **New topics (M16.13, M16.14).** Eleven subjects that had no page: crystal structure and symmetry, relaxation, beyond DFT (GW and RPA), performance and parallelisation, bonding analysis (COHP), Wannier functions and topology, formation energy and the convex hull, molecular dynamics and MLFF, point and charged defects, surfaces and slabs, transition states and NEB.
- **Thin modules deepened.** Module 9 pages expanded; module 10 rewritten as a method-and-inputs walkthrough of the LaCuO3 example with literature and library values only, labelled and cited, and no computed results (D-101, D-120); module 11 tours replaced by guided builds driven by the site tools' own code.
- **One look for every 3D view (D-122).** At the author's request (2026-11-27) every 3D view takes the look of the Studio's VESTA-style viewer: the light rig fixed in the scene, the material finish per object kind, element colours, two-tone 0.10 A bonds, atoms at 0.42 of the covalent radius and a lettered a/b/c axes marker. The data lives in `src/lib/three/vestaStyle.ts`; the engine, `kit.ts`, every scene, `SceneController`, `CellScene`, `SpecScene` and `palette.speciesColors` read it.

## Verification

- `npm run verify`: passed locally (format, lint, typecheck, 3132 unit tests, guards, content and question linters, build, links, size, contrast).
- Size: content pages within 15 KB initial JS; lazy chunk `viewer3d` 14.9 of 45 KB gz (new budget); three 136.6 of 150 KB gz; the largest scene file 17.5 KB gz; all scene files together 171 KB gz, of which a topic loads one.
- Playwright e2e and Lighthouse run in CI (no browser in the build sandbox). `tests/e2e/stage.spec.ts` checks the live canvas, a free drag, keyboard turn and reset, orthographic, steps, Focus 3D and Text only, three.js absent from initial JS, axe, CSP and reduced motion, and saves screenshots that CI uploads (`stage-screenshots`) and prints.

## Known limits

- Some first builds are slow: two-electron scenes in modules 3 and 4 take 80 to 190 ms, the module 8 surface about 100 ms, orbitals 60 to 220 ms and the module 1 scales scene about 115 ms; later builds are memoised or fast.
- A topic page whose lab draws its own canvas can hold two WebGL contexts (lab and stage). The one-canvas rule (D-058) is checked on the design pages only; merging lab canvases into the stage is deferred.
- The local vitest file cache (`node_modules/.vitest-cache`) can keep a stale scene registry after scene files are added; delete it or run with `--no-fsModuleCache`. CI is unaffected.
- Meeting points with the layout branch `claude/hopeful-rubin-adj3m0` are listed in the PR body.
