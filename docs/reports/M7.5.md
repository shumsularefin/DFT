## What was built
- `chgcar.ts`: structure header through the POSCAR reader, grid, x-fastest values, second grid for magnetization, augmentation blocks skipped, progress callback. Helpers: electron count, maximum density, grid index. 120^3 grid (31 MB) parses in about 0.26 s.
- `eigenval.ts` (ISPIN 1 and 2, occupations), `xdatcar.ts` (VASP 5, fixed or variable cell), `doscar.ts` (total and projected, LORBIT 10 and 11, ISPIN 1 and 2, orbital order s, py, pz, px, dxy, dyz, dz2, dxz, dx2). ISPIN in DOSCAR is taken from the total-DOS column count because the fixture writes 0 in the fourth header field with ISPIN 2.
- `bandGap.ts`: VBM, CBM, gap, direct or indirect, direct gap, metallic flag; occupied means occupation at least 0.5.
- `marchingCubes.ts` with the 256-case tables (`marchingCubesTables.ts`, copied from the Bourke tables as shipped in three.js, MIT, in THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md). Edge-welded vertices, linear interpolation, optional periodic wrap, lattice mapping, `meshVolume`, `openEdges`.
