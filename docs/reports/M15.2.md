### M14.3 (planar averages)
- Finding: no pymatgen golden file. The planar average was checked only by a hand-computed test.
  **Fixed:** `scripts/reference/gen_volumetric_reference.py` writes a synthetic LOCPOT (6x5x16, slab-like profile plus seeded noise) and a spin CHGCAR with pymatgen. It reads them back and stores `get_average_along_axis(0, 1, 2)` in `tests/reference/volumetric_reference.json`. `src/lib/physics/volumetric.golden.test.ts` parses the same files with `parseLocpot` / `parseChgcar` and matches all three axes at 1e-9 relative (8 checks). The hand-computed test stays.

### M9.3 (feasibility runtime model)
- Finding: `0.02 * nsites^2.5 * spin 2 * metal 1.5 * 5 steps` core-hours had no source, and the exponent disagreed with the "O(N^3)" text of the size rule.
  **Fixed (D-092):** the estimate is now relative: (nsites / 4)^3, times 2 for spin (S-010, S-019), and the text reads "about N times the cost of a 4-atom non-magnetic cell". The metal factor, step count, cores input and time buckets are gone. The UI line on the project card changed to match (minimal change), and a unit test was added.
- Finding: the bare numbers in `src/lib/mp/` were reviewed. The INCAR values (SIGMA, KSPACING, EDIFFG) come from S-042 presets. The size cut-offs 10 and 30 atoms and the Z > 54 cut-off have no source. **Recorded:** Q-053 (author).
