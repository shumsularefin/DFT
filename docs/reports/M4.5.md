Status: done        Branch: claude/vigilant-newton-ws593b        PR: see docs/PROGRESS.md

## What was built
- `src/lib/crystal/pointgroup.ts`: Delaunay (Selling) reduction, `latticePointGroup`, `hasInversion`. Orders: sc, fcc, bcc and the skewed fcc basis 48; hexagonal 24; rhombohedral 12; tetragonal 16; orthorhombic 8; monoclinic 4; triclinic 2.
- `src/lib/crystal/kmesh.ts`: `meshPoints`, `irreducibleCount` (with weights and time reversal), `meshIsSymmetric` and `meshSymmetryReport` with the Q-017 reason.
- `src/lib/physics/tightbinding2d.ts`: 2D tight-binding toy, optional staggered gap and Gaussian smearing, series erf.
- Lab `kpoint-mesh`: `KmeshLab.tsx`, `KmeshScene.ts` (lazy 3D with Gamma marker, orbit colours), flat slice fallback, `MetalInsulatorPanel.tsx` and `LogErrorPlot.tsx` (log error axis, table alternative), shared `LatticeChooser` and `cells.ts` (the ENCUT lab now uses them). Demo `/design/kpoint-lab/`.

## Gates
- Unit: group orders and basis invariance; all 12 golden spglib irreducible counts (fcc Cu, Gamma-centred and even shifted); all 32 golden `mesh_point_group_symmetry` booleans; exact -8t/pi^2; insulator error below 1e-7 at N = 24 with every ratio below 0.35; metal error above 1e-3 with rising ratios; erf against tabulated values.
- E2E (both projects): 216 points and 16 irreducible for fcc 6x6x6, 28 and the Q-017 text for even Monkhorst-Pack, slider changes, toy panel and table, 3D start/close with one canvas and no three in initial JS, axe in light and dark, 360 px. The ENCUT lab tests still pass after the refactor.
