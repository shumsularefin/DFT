<Section id="why" heading="Why this step comes first">

Every later property is evaluated at this geometry, so an error here propagates everywhere. Experimental
lattice constants are not the DFT minimum: PBE typically overestimates them by 1 to 2%. If you skip the
relaxation and use the experimental cell, the residual stress spoils the elastic and phonon results.

</Section>

<Section id="inputs" heading="The inputs">

<PresetIncar preset="relax" caption="INCAR for the relaxation" />

| Tag    | Setting                                  | Reason                                                                                                                                     |
| ------ | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| ENCUT  | 1.3 × ENMAX                              | Changing the cell changes the number of plane waves at a fixed cutoff. The 30% margin keeps the Pulay stress small.                        |
| ISIF   | 3                                        | Full equilibrium: ions, cell shape and volume. Use 2 for slabs and defects (cell fixed) and 4 for the fixed-volume points of an E–V curve. |
| EDIFFG | −0.005 or −0.001 eV/Å                    | Stops when every force is below \|EDIFFG\|. −0.001 also suits elastic and phonon work.                                                     |
| EDIFF  | 1E-6 (1E-7 to 1E-8 with EDIFFG = −0.001) | Forces are only as accurate as the SCF, so a tight force criterion needs a tight EDIFF.                                                    |
| IBRION | 2, then 1                                | Conjugate gradient (2) is robust far from the minimum. RMM-DIIS (1) is faster close to it but can diverge from a bad start.                |

Use the KPOINTS from your k-point test (VASPkit 102 at a resolution of 0.03 to 0.04) and the POTCAR you
checked in [Prepare the inputs](/workflow/prepare-inputs/).

</Section>

<Section id="run" heading="Run and re-run">

<VaspFile
  name="shell"
  code={`mpirun -np 32 vasp_std > vasp.out
grep "reached required accuracy" OUTCAR      # must appear
grep -c "F=" OSZICAR                         # number of ionic steps taken
grep "external pressure" OUTCAR | tail -1    # should be within ±1-2 kB
cp POSCAR POSCAR.0; cp CONTCAR POSCAR         # repeat until it converges in 1-3 steps`}
/>

Keep the first structure as `POSCAR.0` so you can compare against it later. Copy CONTCAR to POSCAR and
run again until a run converges in one to three ionic steps.

</Section>

<Section id="check" heading="Check before you move on">

- **Lattice constants against experiment.** PBE overestimates by about 1 to 2%, LDA underestimates by about
  1 to 2%, and PBEsol and r2SCAN sit closer. A deviation above about 3% points to a wrong magnetic state,
  missing van der Waals forces (layered materials) or a wrong POTCAR.
- **Symmetry is preserved.** Compare the space groups of `POSCAR.0` and `CONTCAR`: VASPkit 601 on the POSCAR
  and 608 on the relaxed structure, or `phonopy --symmetry -c CONTCAR`.
- **Moments.** `grep "mag=" OSZICAR | tail -1` and the `magnetization (x)` table in OUTCAR.

</Section>
