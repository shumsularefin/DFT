<Why>

A trustworthy DFT study is defined by its reproducible decision chain: every choice from starting geometry to convergence threshold and post-flight check must be documented <Cite id="S-042" />.

</Why>

<Intuition>

<Callout variant="note" title="Results pending: author data not yet supplied">

This summary closes the `RCuO3` worked-example protocol. Quantitative takeaways from the author's VASP outputs will be added once files are placed in `data/author/`.

</Callout>

Across the five stages of this worked example, each step fed directly into the next: tolerance-factor screening prioritized candidate compositions <Cite id="S-001" /> <Cite id="S-002" />, structure checks verified the starting cell, convergence testing locked in `ENCUT` and k-mesh spacing, variable-cell relaxation plus static confirmation established the equilibrium geometry, and fixed-potential non-SCF runs produced the DOS and band structure <Cite id="S-042" />.

<Scene id="perovskite-octahedra" />

You can generate this entire multi-step folder hierarchy and methods checklist automatically for your own material using the <a href="/tools/planner/">Research Planner</a>.

</Intuition>

<TheMath>

Every methods section should report the exact inputs that fix the numerical energy surface: functional, `POTCAR` titles, `ENCUT`, k-mesh spacing, `EDIFF`, `EDIFFG`, smearing (`ISMEAR`, `SIGMA`), and any spin (`ISPIN`, `MAGMOM`) or $+U$ parameters <Cite id="S-042" />.

</TheMath>

<InVasp>

- Archive `INCAR`, `POSCAR`, `KPOINTS`, `CONTCAR`, `OSZICAR`, and key `OUTCAR` metrics for every production run.
- Export the Markdown report from <a href="/tools/review/">Calculation Review</a> straight into your lab notebook.
- Never share licensed `POTCAR` files in public repositories; record their `TITEL` lines instead.

</InVasp>

<WhatChanges>

| Workflow stage       | Output carried forward  | Verification check                           |
| -------------------- | ----------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ------ | ------------------------- |
| Screening            | Candidate stoichiometry | Tolerance factor & oxidation states recorded |
| Convergence          | Locked `ENCUT` & k-mesh | Energy/atom plateau verified                 |
| Relaxation + Static  | `CONTCAR` & `CHGCAR`    | Max force `<                                 | EDIFFG | `, entropy `< 1 meV/atom` |
| Electronic structure | `DOSCAR` & `EIGENVAL`   | Fermi alignment and path labels checked      |

Why document every link in the chain: another researcher (or your future self) can reproduce every number without guessing hidden defaults.

</WhatChanges>

<Mistakes>

<Callout variant="mistake" title="Publishing results without recording POTCAR titles and smearing">

Saying only "we used PBE in VASP" leaves out the PAW variant, `ENCUT`, k-mesh, and smearing width, making the calculation impossible to reproduce reliably.

</Callout>

</Mistakes>
