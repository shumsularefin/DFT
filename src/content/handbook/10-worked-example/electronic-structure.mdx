<Why>

Electronic properties such as metallic vs insulating character, Cu-$3d$/O-$2p$ hybridization, and spin polarization determine how an oxide behaves in devices and catalysis <Cite id="S-042" />.

</Why>

<Intuition>

<Callout variant="note" title="Will be updated: results come from ongoing research">

This page documents the DOS and band-structure protocol for `RCuO3`. Author-computed band gaps, DOS curves, and orbital projections will be added after the research is published. Until then this page shows the method and the inputs only; no result on it is a real or invented number.

</Callout>

Starting from the converged `CHGCAR` of the static run on the standardized primitive cell, run two non-self-consistent (`ICHARG = 11`) follow-up calculations: one on a dense Gamma-centered k-mesh with `ISMEAR = -5` and `LORBIT = 11` for total and projected DOS, and one along the standardized high-symmetry k-path generated by the <a href="/tools/kpath/">k-path tool</a> <Cite id="S-019" /> <Cite id="S-042" />.

<Scene id="reciprocal-fcc" />

Once VASP finishes, drop `DOSCAR` and `EIGENVAL` into the viewers in Module 9 to inspect the Fermi-aligned DOS and band dispersion directly in your browser.

</Intuition>

<TheMath>

Energies are plotted relative to the Fermi level $E_F$ from the dense-mesh static/DOS calculation:

$$\tilde{\varepsilon}_{n\mathbf{k}} = \varepsilon_{n\mathbf{k}} - E_F$$

Always report the exchange-correlation functional and any $+U$ parameters alongside band gaps, because semi-local PBE systematically underestimates semiconductor and insulator gaps <Cite id="S-042" />.

</TheMath>

<InVasp>

- Carry `CHGCAR` from the static SCF run into both the DOS and band-structure folders (`ICHARG = 11`) <Cite id="S-042" />.
- Set `LORBIT = 11` so `DOSCAR` contains $s$, $p$, and $d$ orbital projections for Cu and O <Cite id="S-019" />.
- Use `ISMEAR = -5` for the dense DOS mesh and `ISMEAR = 0` for the line-mode band path.

</InVasp>

<WhatChanges>

| Calculation    | `KPOINTS`                    | `ISMEAR`           | Output inspected |
| -------------- | ---------------------------- | ------------------ | ---------------- |
| Dense DOS/PDOS | Dense Gamma-centered mesh    | `-5` (tetrahedron) | `DOSCAR`         |
| Band structure | Line-mode high-symmetry path | `0` (Gaussian)     | `EIGENVAL`       |

Why separate DOS and band runs: tetrahedron integration requires a 3D k-grid, whereas band structures sample 1D lines.

</WhatChanges>

<Mistakes>

<Callout variant="mistake" title="Using ISMEAR = -5 on a line-mode band KPOINTS file">

The tetrahedron method needs a 3D mesh of k-points and fails on 1D band paths. Switch to `ISMEAR = 0` with small `SIGMA` for band-structure runs.

</Callout>

</Mistakes>
