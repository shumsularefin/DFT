<Why>

Put an insulator in an electric field and two things screen the field. The electron clouds shift a little (fast, electronic screening) and the ions move a little (slow, ionic screening). The <Term id="dielectric-tensor">dielectric tensor</Term> measures both. Along the way VASP computes the <Term id="born-effective-charge">Born effective charges</Term>, which phonopy needs to fix the optical modes of polar crystals and which flag materials close to a ferroelectric instability <Cite id="S-042" />.

</Why>

<Intuition>

The Born effective charge of an atom answers: if I move this atom a little along y, how much does the crystal's polarization change along x? It is a 3 by 3 tensor per atom, a dynamical charge, and it can be far larger than the nominal ionic charge when bonding is strongly covalent. In BaTiO<sub>3</sub>, titanium's Born charge is about +7 against a nominal +4, a sign of strong p-d hybridization and of the ferroelectric instability nearby <Cite id="S-042" />.

The high-frequency tensor, written $\varepsilon_\infty$, is the electronic part alone. Adding the ionic contribution gives the static tensor $\varepsilon_0$ <Cite id="S-042" />.

</Intuition>

<TheMath>

$$\varepsilon_0 = \varepsilon_\infty + \varepsilon_\text{ion}$$

The Born charges obey the acoustic sum rule: moving the whole crystal rigidly cannot polarize it, so for each tensor component the sum over all atoms $\kappa$ is zero, $\sum_\kappa Z^*_{\kappa,\alpha\beta} = 0$. In a real calculation it is only approximately zero <Cite id="S-042" />.

</TheMath>

<InVasp>

The run, on a relaxed unit cell of an insulator or semiconductor <Cite id="S-042" />:

<VaspFile
  name="INCAR"
  code={`PREC     = Accurate
ENCUT    = 520
EDIFF    = 1E-8
ISMEAR   = 0
SIGMA    = 0.01
LREAL    = .FALSE.
LASPH    = .TRUE.
IBRION   = 8
LEPSILON = .TRUE.
LRPA     = .FALSE.
NSW      = 1
LWAVE    = .FALSE.
LCHARG   = .FALSE.`}
/>

- **Why `IBRION = 8` with `LEPSILON`:** density-functional perturbation theory gives the electronic tensor, the ionic contribution and the Gamma phonons in one run <Cite id="S-042" />.
- **Why `LRPA = .FALSE.`:** it includes local-field effects in DFT, which is the tensor you want to report <Cite id="S-042" />.
- **No `NCORE`:** DFPT needs `NCORE = 1`, the default <Cite id="S-042" />.
- **Hybrid or meta-GGA:** replace `IBRION = 8` and `LEPSILON` by `IBRION = 6` and `LCALCEPS = .TRUE.` <Cite id="S-042" />.

**Reading it** <Cite id="S-042" />:

<VaspFile
  name="shell"
  code={`grep -A 5 "MACROSCOPIC STATIC DIELECTRIC TENSOR (including local field effects in DFT)" OUTCAR | tail -4
grep -A 5 "MACROSCOPIC STATIC DIELECTRIC TENSOR IONIC CONTRIBUTION" OUTCAR
grep -A 40 "BORN EFFECTIVE CHARGES" OUTCAR | head -45
phonopy-vasp-born vasprun.xml > BORN`}
/>

The first line is $\varepsilon_\infty$, the second the ionic part, the third the Born charges per ion; the last writes the `BORN` file for phonopy's non-analytical correction. The site's OUTCAR reader takes the same three blocks and checks the sum rule; it is tested on files that pymatgen reads back.

</InVasp>

<WhatChanges>

| Check                                                        | Good sign                                | If not                                                                      |
| ------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Sum of Z\* over all atoms, per component                     | Below about 0.1                          | The k-mesh or EDIFF is too loose (Heuristic level) <Cite id="S-042" />      |
| Z\* compared with the nominal charge                         | Similar                                  | Much larger: strong covalency, possibly near a ferroelectric instability    |
| $\varepsilon_\infty$ against $\varepsilon_1(0)$ from LOPTICS | Close; LEPSILON usually slightly smaller | LEPSILON includes local-field effects, LOPTICS does not <Cite id="S-042" /> |

</WhatChanges>

<Mistakes>

<Callout variant="mistake" title="Running it on a metal">

The dielectric tensor, the Born charges and the polarization are ill-defined in a metal: free carriers screen any static field completely. Do not run this on a metallic state <Cite id="S-042" />.

</Callout>

<Callout variant="mistake" title="Reading the block without local fields">

OUTCAR prints more than one dielectric tensor. The one to report is the block "including local field effects in DFT".

</Callout>

</Mistakes>
