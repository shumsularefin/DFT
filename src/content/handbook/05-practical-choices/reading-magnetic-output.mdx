<Why>

After a spin-polarized VASP job finishes, the total energy alone does not tell you what magnetic state the calculation actually found. You have to read three pieces of output: the total cell moment in `OSZICAR`, the atom-by-atom moment table in `OUTCAR`, and (for electronic structure) the up-spin and down-spin densities of states in `DOSCAR` <Cite id="S-041" />.

</Why>

<Intuition>

1. **`OSZICAR` (`mag=`):** At the end of each ionic step, `OSZICAR` prints `mag=` followed by the net magnetic moment of the whole simulation cell in $\mu_B$. For a ferromagnet, `mag` is roughly the sum of the site moments. For a clean antiferromagnet, `mag` is close to `0.000`, which by itself cannot distinguish an antiferromagnet from a collapsed non-magnetic state!
2. **`OUTCAR` (`magnetization (x)`):** When `LORBIT = 11` is set, `OUTCAR` prints a table listing the $s, p, d, f$ and total local magnetic moment inside each atom's PAW projection sphere <Cite id="S-041" />. Always read the **last** occurrence of this table (the converged ionic step). Here an antiferromagnet shows large opposite-sign moments on its magnetic sites (for example, `+3.8` and `-3.8`), whereas a collapsed non-magnetic run shows zeros everywhere.
3. **Spin-polarized DOS (`DOSCAR`):** With `ISPIN = 2`, the density of states is split into up-spin and down-spin channels. Plotting up-spin positive and down-spin negative (mirrored across zero) makes spin splitting visible at a glance: symmetric mirrors mean zero net polarization, while shifted peaks reveal exchange splitting <Cite id="S-059" />.

<Scene id="fm-cubic" />

Note that the sum of atomic moments in `OUTCAR` rarely equals `OSZICAR` `mag=` exactly: the `OUTCAR` numbers only integrate inside atomic projection spheres, leaving out the interstitial space between atoms <Cite id="S-065" />.

</Intuition>

<TheMath>

The total cell moment reported in `OSZICAR` is the exact integral over the entire unit cell $\Omega$:

$$M_{\text{tot}} = \int_\Omega \bigl(n_\uparrow(\mathbf r) - n_\downarrow(\mathbf r)\bigr)\,d^3r$$

Each atomic moment $m_i$ in the `OUTCAR` `magnetization (x)` table is the projection inside a sphere of radius $R_i$ centred on ion $i$:

$$m_i = \int_{|\mathbf r - \mathbf R_i| \le R_i} \bigl(n_\uparrow(\mathbf r) - n_\downarrow(\mathbf r)\bigr)\,d^3r$$

Because the spheres do not fill all of $\Omega$, $M_{\text{tot}} - \sum_i m_i = M_{\text{interstitial}}$ is generally non-zero <Cite id="S-065" />.

</TheMath>

<InVasp>

```incar
ISPIN = 2
MAGMOM = 2*4.0 2*-4.0 16*0.0
LORBIT = 11
```

- Check `OSZICAR` first to confirm electronic convergence and inspect the net `mag=` value <Cite id="S-041" />.
- Search `OUTCAR` for the final `magnetization (x)` block to verify every magnetic site's sign and magnitude <Cite id="S-041" />.
- Drop your `OUTCAR` and `OSZICAR` files into the Analyse tab of the [Magnetic configuration tool](/tools/magnetic/) to parse the table and check automatically whether each run kept its intended pattern.

</InVasp>

<WhatChanges>

| Output check          | Healthy FM run                  | Healthy AFM run                      | Collapsed non-magnetic run |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ | -------------------------- |
| `OSZICAR` `mag=`      | Large non-zero total            | Near `0.000`                         | `0.000`                    |
| `OUTCAR` site moments | All magnetic sites same sign    | Equal and opposite signs on sites    | All sites near `0.000`     |
| Mirrored DOS          | Up and down shifted differently | Total DOS symmetric, site PDOS split | Up and down identical      |

Why check `OUTCAR` and not just `OSZICAR`: `OSZICAR` `mag = 0` looks identical for an antiferromagnet and a collapsed non-magnetic state.

</WhatChanges>

<Mistakes>

<Callout variant="mistake" title="Reading the first magnetization table in OUTCAR">

During a geometry relaxation, `OUTCAR` prints a `magnetization (x)` block at every ionic step. Always inspect the last block in the file.

</Callout>

<Callout variant="mistake" title="Forgetting LORBIT = 11">

Without `LORBIT = 11` (or `10`), VASP does not write the per-site magnetization table to `OUTCAR`, leaving you unable to confirm an antiferromagnetic pattern.

</Callout>

</Mistakes>
