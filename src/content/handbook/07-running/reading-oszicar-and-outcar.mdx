<Why>

VASP rarely stops with an error when something goes wrong. It finishes, writes numbers, and leaves it to you to notice that a step never converged or that the forces are still large. Reading OSZICAR and OUTCAR is how you notice. Four checks, a minute each, separate a result from a guess.

</Why>

<Intuition>

<Term id="oszicar">OSZICAR</Term> has two kinds of lines <Cite id="S-041" />:

- One line per **electronic step**, starting with the algorithm: `DAV` (blocked Davidson), `RMM` (RMM-DIIS) or `CG`. The columns are the step number N, the free energy E, its change dE since the previous step, the change in the band-structure energy d eps, ncg, and the residuals rms and rms(c).
- One summary line per **ionic step**: `F` (free energy), `E0` (energy extrapolated to zero smearing), `dE` (change since the previous ionic step) and, with spin, `mag` (total moment in Bohr magnetons).

dE should shrink by orders of magnitude within each ionic step. When it falls below EDIFF, the electronic loop stops.

<Term id="outcar">OUTCAR</Term> is long. Find these lines first, with grep <Cite id="S-042" />:

- `reached required accuracy`: the relaxation met EDIFFG.
- `TOTAL-FORCE`: the force table, eV per angstrom.
- `free  energy   TOTEN` and `energy without entropy`: their difference is the entropy term.
- `E-fermi`, `external pressure`, `magnetization (x)`, `Elapsed time`.

<Level min="intermediate">

**The four checks** <Cite id="S-041" /> <Cite id="S-042" />:

1. **Did every electronic loop converge?** An ionic step that used NELM electronic steps (default 60) did not converge. VASP continues anyway, and the forces from that step are unreliable.
2. **Did the relaxation stop for the right reason?** "reached required accuracy" must be in OUTCAR. Without it the run stopped at NSW or was killed.
3. **Are the forces small?** With a negative EDIFFG, the largest force must be below |EDIFFG|.
4. **Is the smearing small enough?** The entropy term T\*S per atom should be below about 1 meV (Heuristic); otherwise reduce SIGMA.

</Level>

<Level min="pro">

A complete run ends with timing lines ("Elapsed time"). If they are missing, the job is still going, crashed, or hit the wall-time limit. For a variable-cell relaxation, `external pressure` near zero (within a few kB) and a small Pulay stress mean the cell is converged; a large Pulay stress means ENCUT is too low for the stress <Cite id="S-041" />.

</Level>

</Intuition>

<Lab id="output-reader" title="Output reader" />

<TheMath>

The entropy term is the difference between two OUTCAR energies,

$$T S = E_\text{without entropy} - F_\text{TOTEN},$$

and the check is $|T S| / N_\text{atoms} < 1$ meV <Cite id="S-042" />. For Gaussian smearing, E0 in OSZICAR is the average of the energy and the free energy, $E_0 = \tfrac12 (E + F)$, which removes the leading error in SIGMA <Cite id="S-019" />. The force check compares $\max_I |\mathbf{F}_I|$ over all atoms with $|\text{EDIFFG}|$.

</TheMath>

<InVasp>

The greps the Workflow Handbook lists for a quick look <Cite id="S-042" />:

<VaspFile
  name="shell"
  code={`grep "reached required accuracy" OUTCAR
grep -A $((NIONS+2)) "TOTAL-FORCE" OUTCAR | tail -n $NIONS
grep "free  energy   TOTEN" OUTCAR | tail -1
grep "energy without entropy" OUTCAR | tail -1
grep "in kB" OUTCAR | tail -1
grep "external pressure" OUTCAR | tail -1
grep -A 8 "magnetization (x)" OUTCAR | tail -8
grep E-fermi OUTCAR
grep "Elapsed time" OUTCAR`}
/>

<WhatChanges>

| If you see...                            | ...it means                                                       |
| ---------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| An ionic step with NELM electronic steps | That step did not converge; fix the mixing before trusting forces |
| No "reached required accuracy"           | The relaxation is not finished: rerun from CONTCAR                |
| T\*S per atom of several meV             | SIGMA is too large for this system                                |
| dE jumping up and down without shrinking | Charge sloshing: module 8 covers the fixes                        |
| `mag` drifting to 0 in a magnetic system | The moment collapsed: check MAGMOM and the starting guess         |

Why these checks: each one catches a failure that VASP does not report as an error. What happens if you skip them: unconverged numbers in your results table.

</WhatChanges>

<Mistakes>

<Callout variant="mistake" title="Quoting F for a Gaussian-smeared run">
  For ISMEAR = 0, quote E0 (sigma to 0), not F, when comparing energies <Cite id="S-019" />.
</Callout>

<Callout variant="mistake" title="Reading the first force table">
  OUTCAR has one TOTAL-FORCE table per ionic step. The last one belongs to the final structure.
</Callout>

</Mistakes>
