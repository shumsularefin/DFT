<Why>

Bad starting geometries such as overlapping atoms, polar uncompensated slabs, or tiny vacuum boxes are one of the fastest ways to waste VASP jobs <Cite id="S-041" />.

</Why>

<Intuition>

The <a href="/builder/">Material Builder</a> combines two browser-only workspaces: **Crystal mode** for periodic solids (templates, supercells, Miller-index slabs, vacancies, substitutions, and strain) and **Molecule mode** for 2D sketching, 3D valence clean-up, and molecule-in-a-box `POSCAR` export.

<Scene id="fcc" />

In Crystal mode, a live chemistry-and-geometry validity panel runs rules `B001` through `B012` on every edit, flagging overlapping atoms, unrelaxed cell collapses, or thin vacuum before you export `POSCAR` or `CIF` <Cite id="S-021" />. Every edit supports undo/redo and one-click hand-off straight into the Input Builder, k-path tool, or Magnetic Configuration tool.

</Intuition>

<TheMath>

Overlap checks compare every pair distance $d_{ij}$ (under periodic minimum-image convention) against the sum of Cordero single-bond covalent radii <Cite id="S-021" />:

$$d_{ij} < f_{\mathrm{min}}\,(r_{\mathrm{cov}, i} + r_{\mathrm{cov}, j})$$

Flagging severe overlaps before exporting prevents immediate VASP `ZHEGV` or ionic-step blow-ups.

</TheMath>

<InVasp>

- Build or drop a `POSCAR`/`CIF` in <a href="/builder/">Material Builder</a> and resolve all error-level validity warnings before exporting.
- For slabs and molecules in a box, keep vacuum padding $\ge 10$ to $15\text{ \AA}$ and use `ISIF = 2` (fixed cell) in VASP <Cite id="S-041" />.
- Click **Send to Input Builder** or **Send to k-path** to keep the exact structure across tools without re-uploading.

</InVasp>

<WhatChanges>

| Builder mode  | Core operations                                           | Export formats                |
| ------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| Crystal mode  | Supercell, slab, vacancy, substitute, strain, standardize | `POSCAR`, `CIF`, hand-off     |
| Molecule mode | 2D sketch, bond order, formal charge, 3D valence clean-up | `XYZ`, `MOL`, `POSCAR` in box |

Why validate in the browser: geometric bugs are caught in milliseconds before touching an HPC queue.

</WhatChanges>

<Mistakes>

<Callout variant="mistake" title="Treating the 3D molecule clean-up as a DFT-optimized geometry">

Molecule mode's 3D clean-up uses a fast valence-geometry heuristic to produce a clean starting geometry; always relax the molecule in VASP (or a quantum-chemistry code) before computing properties.

</Callout>

</Mistakes>
