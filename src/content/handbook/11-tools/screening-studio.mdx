<Why>

When exploring a chemical family, filtering a structured catalog by stability, band gap, element constraints, and cell size lets you focus VASP compute on the most promising candidates <Cite id="S-024" />.

</Why>

<Intuition>

The <a href="/studio/">Screening Studio</a> runs entirely in your browser: it loads a compact columnar dataset, filters tens of thousands of entries in milliseconds, and exports shortlists to CSV. Clicking any candidate opens its <a href="/studio/project/">Project Card</a> with a transparent Green/Yellow/Red feasibility rubric, runtime bucket, and a three-step starter `INCAR` sequence <Cite id="S-042" />.

<Scene id="perovskite-octahedra" />

Check the banner at the top of the Studio: until the author runs a real Materials Project data pull, the Studio clearly labels its table as synthetic demo data so demonstration numbers are never confused with real materials entries <Cite id="S-024" />.

</Intuition>

<TheMath>

Thermodynamic stability against competing phases is tracked by the energy above the convex hull $E_{\mathrm{hull}}$ (in eV/atom). Compounds with $E_{\mathrm{hull}} = 0$ lie on the zero-temperature hull; small positive values may still be synthetically accessible depending on kinetics and temperature <Cite id="S-024" />.

</TheMath>

<InVasp>

- Filter by elements, band-gap window, $E_{\mathrm{hull}}$, and atom count in <a href="/studio/">Screening Studio</a>.
- Open a candidate's <a href="/studio/project/">Project Card</a> to inspect its feasibility flags and download starter `INCAR` files.
- Always confirm magnetic ground states and PAW choices in VASP rather than relying solely on database metadata <Cite id="S-042" />.

</InVasp>

<WhatChanges>

| Studio filter        | What it narrows         | Practical effect                                 |
| -------------------- | ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| `E_hull` max         | Thermodynamic stability | Drops highly unstable polymorphs early           |
| Max sites (`nsites`) | Supercell cost          | Keeps starter runs within modest HPC wall-time   |
| Band gap range       | Electronic class        | Separates metals, semiconductors, and insulators |

Why screen before simulating: a two-minute filter prevents spending weeks relaxing unstable or prohibitively large cells.

</WhatChanges>

<Mistakes>

<Callout variant="mistake" title="Treating database band gaps as experimental values">

High-throughput catalogs such as the Materials Project primarily use semi-local GGA/GGA+U functionals, which systematically underestimate band gaps relative to experiment.

</Callout>

</Mistakes>
