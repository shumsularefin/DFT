<Why>

A relaxation job that exits cleanly without an error message has not necessarily converged: it may simply have hit `NSW` steps while forces are still large, or relaxed into a much larger volume where the fixed plane-wave basis is no longer accurate <Cite id="S-042" />.

</Why>

<Intuition>

Before accepting `CONTCAR` as your relaxed structure, check four items:

1. `OUTCAR` contains `reached required accuracy` (meaning every ionic force fell below `|EDIFFG|`).
2. Every electronic loop along the way converged before hitting `NELM`.
3. The trajectory in `XDATCAR` settled smoothly rather than oscillating back and forth.
4. If `ISIF = 3` changed the cell volume by more than a few percent, copy `CONTCAR` to `POSCAR` and run a second relaxation so VASP rebuilds the plane-wave basis at the new cell volume <Cite id="S-042" />.

</Intuition>

<Lab id="relaxation-viewer" title="Relaxation trajectory viewer" />

<TheMath>

The maximum ionic force is the largest Euclidean norm across all $N$ atoms:

$$F_{\max} = \max_{1 \le i \le N} \|\mathbf F_i\|_2$$

Setting a negative `EDIFFG` (for example `EDIFFG = -0.02`) stops ionic relaxation only when $F_{\max} < |\text{EDIFFG}|$ in eV/A <Cite id="S-019" />.

</TheMath>

<InVasp>

- Always inspect `CONTCAR` (not `POSCAR`) for the final relaxed coordinates <Cite id="S-042" />.
- After an `ISIF = 3` relaxation, run a final static calculation (`NSW = 0`, `IBRION = -1`) on `CONTCAR` to get a clean total energy without Pulay stress drift.

</InVasp>

<WhatChanges>

| Observation in output       | Likely meaning            | Action                                               |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | --- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| `reached required accuracy` | Forces converged below `  | EDIFFG                                               | `   | Check volume change, then run static SCF on `CONTCAR` |
| Stopped at step `NSW`       | Relaxation unfinished     | Copy `CONTCAR` to `POSCAR` and continue              |
| Volume changed by > 10 %    | Plane-wave basis is stale | Re-run relaxation from `CONTCAR` <Cite id="S-042" /> |

Why restart after a large volume change: VASP fixes the set of plane waves at the initial cell volume.

</WhatChanges>

<Mistakes>

<Callout variant="mistake" title="Using a positive EDIFFG without realising it stops on energy, not force">

A positive `EDIFFG` (e.g. `1E-4`) stops when the energy difference between ionic steps drops below that value, even if individual atomic forces are still large. Use negative `EDIFFG` for force convergence <Cite id="S-019" />.

</Callout>

</Mistakes>
