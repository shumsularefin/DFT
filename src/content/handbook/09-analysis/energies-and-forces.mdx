<Why>

A finished VASP calculation prints three different energies at every step (`TOTEN`, `energy without entropy`, and `energy(sigma->0)`). Picking the wrong one or comparing raw energies computed with different `POTCAR` files or `ENCUT` values produces meaningless numbers <Cite id="S-042" />.

</Why>

<Intuition>

Total energies in plane-wave DFT are only meaningful as **differences** between calculations that share the same functional, `POTCAR` files, `ENCUT`, and compatible k-point sampling. For finite-temperature smearing (such as Gaussian or Methfessel-Paxton), `energy(sigma->0)` extrapolates out the artificial electronic entropy term and is the standard energy to report for ground-state comparisons <Cite id="S-042" />.

<Scene id="fcc" />

Forces on each atom (printed in `OUTCAR` under `TOTAL-FORCE (eV/Angst)`) tell you whether atoms are at equilibrium. For a converged geometry relaxation, the largest force magnitude should fall below `|EDIFFG|` (typically `0.01` to `0.02` eV/A).

</Intuition>

<TheMath>

With electronic smearing, the free energy $F = E - T S$ (`TOTEN`) differs from the zero-temperature energy $E$. The extrapolated energy `energy(sigma->0)` is:

$$E(\sigma \to 0) = \frac{1}{2}\bigl(F + E_{\text{without entropy}}\bigr)$$

Always check that $|T S| / N_{\text{atoms}} < 1\text{ meV/atom}$ <Cite id="S-042" />.

</TheMath>

<InVasp>

- Read `E0` from `OSZICAR` (which equals `energy(sigma->0)` in `OUTCAR`) for energy comparisons <Cite id="S-042" />.
- Check the final `TOTAL-FORCE (eV/Angst)` block in `OUTCAR` to confirm every force component is below `|EDIFFG|` <Cite id="S-019" />.
- Normalize energies per atom or per formula unit before comparing cells of different sizes.

</InVasp>

<WhatChanges>

| Quantity in `OUTCAR`     | Meaning                                  | When to use                                                    |
| ------------------------ | ---------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- | --- | ---------- |
| `free energy TOTEN`      | Free energy $F = E - T S$                | Variational quantity used to compute Hellmann-Feynman forces   |
| `energy without entropy` | $E$ before $\sigma \to 0$ extrapolation  | Compare with `TOTEN` to check $                                | T S | $ per atom |
| `energy(sigma->0)`       | Extrapolated zero-smearing energy (`E0`) | Report for ground-state energy differences <Cite id="S-042" /> |

Why report `energy(sigma->0)`: removes the artificial smearing entropy offset while keeping forces consistent with `TOTEN`.

</WhatChanges>

<Mistakes>

<Callout variant="mistake" title="Comparing raw energies across different ENCUT or POTCARs">

Plane-wave total energies shift by tens of eV if you change `ENCUT`, `POTCAR` variants (for example `Ti` vs `Ti_pv`), or functionals. Only differences with matched settings are physical.

</Callout>

</Mistakes>
